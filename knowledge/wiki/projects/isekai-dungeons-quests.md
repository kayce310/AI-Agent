└─ Special: Tutorial dungeon

Slime Cave

├─ Recommended Party: Solo

├─ Boss: Queen Slime (HP: 80)

├─ Respawn: 1 hour

## 📈 PROGRESSION SYSTEM

### Experience Scaling
```
Lvl 1:   100 EXP to Lvl 2
Lvl 5:   500 EXP to Lvl 6 (5x)
Lvl 10:  1000 EXP to Lvl 11 (10x)
Lvl 20:  5000 EXP to Lvl 21 (50x)
Lvl 30:  10000 EXP to Lvl 31 (100x)
Lvl 50:  50000 EXP to Lvl 51 (500x)
Lvl 80:  100000 EXP to Lvl 81 (1000x)

Total EXP to Max (Lvl 100):
Lvl 1-20: ~50K EXP (early game)
Lvl 21-50: ~300K EXP (mid game)
Lvl 51-80: ~2M EXP (late game)
Lvl 81-100: ~5M EXP (endgame)
```

### Stat Growth Per Level
```
Default Growth:
├─ HP: +10 per level
├─ Mana: +5 per level
├─ ATK: +2 per level
├─ DEF: +1 per level
└─ SPD: +0.5 per level (soft cap at 15)

Bonus at Level Milestones:
├─ Lvl 10: +50 Max HP, New skill slot
├─ Lvl 20: +100 Max HP, +50 Max Mana
├─ Lvl 30: +150 Max HP, New skill
├─ Lvl 50: +250 Max HP, Prestige option
├─ Lvl 80: +500 Max HP, Ascension mechanic
└─ Lvl 100: Max level reached, New Game+ available
```

### Prestige System (At Lvl 50)
```
Prestige Reset:
├─ Option: Reset to Lvl 1 (keep items, gold)
├─ Reward: Prestige Rank +1
├─ Bonus: +2% EXP gain per prestige (max 10 prestiges = 20% bonus)
├─ New Title: "Prestige X" (cosmetic)
└─ Cost: 10000G

Benefits:
├─ 2x EXP gain until Lvl 50 again
├─ Special equipment for prestigers only
├─ Prestige-only quests (+100% rewards)
└─ Leaderboard: Prestige ranking
```

### Ascension Mechanic (At Lvl 80)
```
Ascension Path:
├─ Requirement: Lvl 80 + 1 Mythic Item + 100000G
├─ Effect: Level stays 80, but +100% base stats
├─ New Rank: "Ascended Warrior" (display name change)
├─ Skill Boost: All skills +50% effectiveness
└─ Cooldown: 7 days until next ascension

Stacking:
├─ Ascension 1: +100% base stats
├─ Ascension 2: +200% base stats (total)
├─ Ascension 3: +300% base stats (total)
├─ Max Ascension: 5 (total +500% stats)
```

### Skill Trees

#### Warrior Tree (Melee Focus)
```
Tier 1 (Lvl 5):
├─ Power Strike: Deal 150% damage (+20 mana)
├─ Block: Reduce incoming damage by 50% (5s cooldown)
└─ Cleave: Hit 3 enemies in front (30 mana)

Tier 2 (Lvl 20):
├─ Whirlwind: Spin attack, hit all around (50 mana)
├─ Armor Mastery: +25% defense passive
└─ Execution: Instant kill if enemy <20% HP (30s CD)

Tier 3 (Lvl 40):
├─ God Slayer: +100% damage to bosses (60 mana, 60s CD)
├─ Immortal Guard: Cannot be killed for 5 seconds (100 mana, 120s CD)
└─ War Cry: +50% ATK for party for 10s (40 mana)

Tier 4 (Lvl 80):
├─ Titan's Wrath: 300% damage to all (100 mana, 180s CD)
├─ Last Stand: Survive killing blow (1 per hour)
└─ Eternal Warrior: +200% stats for 30s (150 mana, 300s CD)
```

#### Mage Tree (Magic Focus)
```
Tier 1 (Lvl 5):
├─ Fireball: Deal 100 fire damage (15 mana)
├─ Ice Bolt: Freeze enemy 2s (12 mana)
└─ Lightning: 80 damage + chain 3 enemies (18 mana)

Tier 2 (Lvl 20):
├─ Meteor: 200 damage to area (40 mana)
├─ Mana Shield: 50% damage blocked to mana (30 mana passive)
└─ Teleport: Move instantly (25 mana, 10s CD)

Tier 3 (Lvl 40):
├─ Time Slow: Reduce enemy speed by 80% (60 mana)
├─ Mana Explosion: 300 damage (80 mana, 30s CD)
└─ Spell Mastery: +50% spell damage (passive)

Tier 4 (Lvl 80):
├─ Forbidden Spell: Instant kill 50% chance (150 mana, 120s CD)
├─ Mana Surge: +500 mana instantly (120 mana)
└─ Dimensional Rift: Summon rift, damage all in area (200 mana, 300s CD)
```

#### Rogue Tree (Speed Focus)
```
Tier 1 (Lvl 5):
├─ Quick Strike: 120% damage + 2 hits (10 mana)
├─ Dodge: Evade next attack (15 mana, 8s CD)
└─ Poison Dart: Apply poison damage over 5s (12 mana)

Tier 2 (Lvl 20):
├─ Backstab: 200% damage if unseen (25 mana)
├─ Shadow Clone: Summon clone for 10s (35 mana)
└─ Evasion: Dodge 60% of damage (30 mana passive)

Tier 3 (Lvl 40):
├─ Assassination: Instant kill weak enemies (50 mana, 20s CD)
├─ Death Mark: Mark enemy, +100% damage taken for 10s (40 mana)
└─ Smoke Bomb: Escape + 5s invisibility (30 mana, 30s CD)

Tier 4 (Lvl 80):
├─ Shadow Dance: 5 hits in 3 seconds (100 mana, 60s CD)
├─ Deathblow: Guaranteed crit + 400% damage (120 mana, 120s CD)
└─ Perfect Form: Dodge 90% damage for 5s (150 mana, 300s CD)
```
