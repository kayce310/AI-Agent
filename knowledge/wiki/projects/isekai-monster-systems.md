- **Hell Hounds** (Rank E) — HP: 80, ATK: 12, DROP: Hound Fang, Lava Stone

- **Prestige System**: Reset for 2x rewards

#### 💎 Vật Phẩm Chính
- **Hell Crystals** (등급별): Common(C), Rare(B), Epic(A), Legendary(S)

### ⚙️ FARMING STRATEGY

#### Cho Newbie (Lvl 1-20)
1. Farm **Goblins** & **Slimes** → Get 100G for first equipment
2. Buy **Wooden Sword** + **Leather Armor**
3. Move to **Dire Wolves** at lvl 10+

#### Cho Mid-Game (Lvl 21-50)
1. Hunt **Elf Soldiers** & **Demon Knights** for cores
2. Collect **Spirit Stones** untuk craft upgrades
3. Join group dungeons for faster progression

#### Cho End-Game (Lvl 51+)
1. Farm **Boss Monsters** with party
2. Collect **Legendary/Mythic** drops for optimal gear
3. Hunt **Shadow Sovereign Portals** for **Mythic items**
