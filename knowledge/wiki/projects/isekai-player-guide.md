## 💡 CLASS GUIDE

### Warrior Guide

#### Playstyle
```
Role: Tank/Melee DPS
Strategy: Absorb damage, generate threat, CC enemies
Strengths:
├─ Highest HP pool (+25% vs others)
├─ High DEF stat (Warrior DEF: 10 base vs Mage: 3)
├─ Crowd control abilities (Stun, Knockback)
├─ Single-target burst damage (Power Strike 150% damage)
└─ Block mechanic (50% damage reduction, tactical)

Weaknesses:
├─ Low movement speed (-20% vs base)
├─ Low mana pool (limits spell casting)
├─ Limited AoE damage (mostly single-target)
├─ Kite vulnerability (slow movement = vulnerable to ranged)
└─ Less versatile (mainly tank role)

Optimal Skill Build (Lvl 1-20):
├─ Lvl 1: Power Strike (150% single damage)
├─ Lvl 5: Block (50% damage reduction)
├─ Lvl 10: Cleave (hit 3 enemies)
├─ Lvl 15: Armor Mastery (passive +25% DEF)
└─ Lvl 20: Whirlwind (AoE damage 2x area)

Gear Progression:
├─ Lvl 1-5: Wooden Sword + Cloth Robe (starter)
├─ Lvl 6-15: Iron Sword + Leather Armor
├─ Lvl 16-25: Steel Blade + Chain Mail
├─ Lvl 26-50: Mythril Sword + Mithril Plate
└─ Lvl 50+: Dragon Fang + Dragon Scale (epic tier)

Recommended Playstyle:
├─ Solo: Farm tough enemies (2-3 levels lower)
├─ Group: Be the tank (absorb damage, protect allies)
├─ Dungeon: Tank role in raids
├─ PvP: Sustained battle, outlast opponents
└─ Farming: Slow but safe, very tanky
```

### Mage Guide

#### Playstyle
```
Role: Magic DPS/Crowd Control
Strategy: Deal high damage, control battlefield, support team
Strengths:
├─ Highest mana pool (100 base = 4x Warrior's)
├─ Best crowd control (Freeze, Slow, Teleport)
├─ AoE damage (Meteor hits entire area)
├─ Range advantage (30 units range vs 10 melee)
├─ Utility spells (Teleport, buffs, debuffs)
└─ Highest damage ceiling (80-100 DPS potential)

Weaknesses:
├─ Lowest HP pool (-25% vs base)
├─ Lowest DEF stat (base 3 DEF = fragile)
├─ Mana-dependent (no mana = cannot attack)
├─ Cast times (1-3 second delay = vulnerable)
├─ High positioning requirement (must stay safe)
└─ Complex rotation (requires skill management)

Optimal Skill Build (Lvl 1-20):
├─ Lvl 1: Fireball (100 fire damage, single target)
├─ Lvl 5: Ice Bolt (freeze 2s, single target)
├─ Lvl 10: Lightning (80 damage + chain 3 enemies)
├─ Lvl 15: Mana Shield (50% to mana, passive)
└─ Lvl 20: Meteor (200 damage, all enemies in area)

Gear Progression:
├─ Lvl 1-5: Wooden Staff + Robe (starter)
├─ Lvl 6-15: Iron Staff + Robe (INT scaling)
├─ Lvl 16-25: Mithril Staff + Robes
├─ Lvl 26-50: Dragon Staff + Mystical Robes
└─ Lvl 50+: Legendary Grimoire + Celestial Robes

Recommended Playstyle:
├─ Solo: Burst down enemies before they reach you
├─ Group: Back-line DPS, crowd control adds
├─ Dungeon: Primary damage dealer
├─ PvP: Kite & burst, maintain distance
├─ Farming: High damage = fast clear, high attention needed
└─ Tip: Never get close (positioning is survival)
```

### Rogue Guide

#### Playstyle
```
Role: Burst DPS/Skirmisher
Strategy: Quick strikes, high burst, escape when needed
Strengths:
├─ Highest movement speed (+30% vs base)
├─ High crit rate (+10% base crit chance)
├─ Quick attack speed (multiple hits fast)
├─ Evasion mechanics (Dodge skill evades attack)
├─ Stealth capability (partial invisibility)
├─ High burst potential (multiple crit hits in combo)
└─ Mid-range flexibility (can melee or semi-ranged)

Weaknesses:
├─ Medium HP (normal health pool)
├─ Medium DEF (normal defense = squishy)
├─ Range-dependent (must be within 10 units)
├─ High-risk playstyle (risky = high reward)
├─ Requires precise timing (skill ceiling high)
└─ Less forgiving (mistakes = death)

Optimal Skill Build (Lvl 1-20):
├─ Lvl 1: Quick Strike (120% damage x2 hits)
├─ Lvl 5: Dodge (evade next attack completely)
├─ Lvl 10: Poison Dart (apply poison DoT)
├─ Lvl 15: Evasion (passive +60% dodge rate)
└─ Lvl 20: Backstab (200% damage if unseen)

Gear Progression:
├─ Lvl 1-5: Dagger + Leather Armor (fast, light)
├─ Lvl 6-15: Dual Daggers + Leather Armor
├─ Lvl 16-25: Curved Swords + Leather Suit
├─ Lvl 26-50: Mythril Daggers + Leather Plate
└─ Lvl 50+: Assassin's Blades + Shadow Suit (epic)

Recommended Playstyle:
├─ Solo: Burst damage, kite if needed
├─ Group: Off-DPS, secondary damage dealer
├─ Dungeon: Burst adds down, avoid big hits
├─ PvP: Burst opponent, escape, reposition, repeat
├─ Farming: High APM (actions per minute), fast clears
└─ Tip: Speed & positioning > tanking (don't face-tank)
```

## 🎯 COMBAT MECHANICS QUICK TIPS

### Essential Combat Knowledge

#### Damage Mitigation
```
Formula: Damage = (ATK - Enemy DEF) × Skill Multiplier ± 10%

Example:
├─ Your ATK: 50
├─ Enemy DEF: 20
├─ Power Strike: 150% multiplier
└─ Damage: (50-20) × 1.5 = 45 damage ±5 range

Defense Scaling:
├─ 5 DEF = 5% reduction
├─ 10 DEF = 9% reduction
├─ 50 DEF = 33% reduction
├─ 100 DEF = 50% reduction
└─ Soft cap: 200 DEF = 67% reduction (practical max)

Tip: Every 10 DEF saves ~5 damage per hit (scales diminishing)
```

#### Critical Strikes
```
Base Crit Chance: 5% (all players)
Scaling: +1% per 10 ATK
├─ 50 ATK = 5% + 5% = 10% crit chance
├─ 100 ATK = 5% + 10% = 15% crit chance
├─ 200 ATK = 5% + 20% = 25% crit chance
└─ Hard cap: 95% (diminishing returns after 85%)

Crit Damage: 1.5x base damage
├─ Normal hit: 45 damage
├─ Critical hit: 67.5 damage (+50%)
└─ Benefit: Huge damage spikes, unreliable for planning

Tip: Don't prioritize crit early; consistency beats variance
```

#### Status Effects Resistance
```
Resistance calculation: Reduces effect duration
├─ 0% resistance = Full duration
├─ 50% resistance = 50% of duration
├─ 100% resistance = Immune (no effect)
└─ Example: Poison (5s) with 50% resist = 2.5s duration

Building Resistance:
├─ Equipment: Resistances stack
├─ Buffs: +X% status resistance (temporary)
├─ Passive skills: Permanent bonus
└─ End-game: 100+ resistance possible (full immunity)

Tip: Elemental resistance matters in specific content (volcano = fire res)
```

## 🆘 COMMON QUESTIONS

### Frequently Asked Questions
```
Q: Can I solo endgame dungeons?
A: Most content requires 3-8 players. Solo dungeons available 
   for casual players (scaled down). Boss raids require groups.

Q: How long to reach max level?
A: ~100-150 hours for casual play, 40-60 hours for efficient 
   farming (no story, pure leveling).

Q: Is this pay-to-win?
A: NO. Zero progression advantages from money. Only cosmetics 
   can be purchased. All players on equal footing (skill matters).

Q: Can I trade items with friends?
A: Yes! Direct trading available (cooldown between trades). 
   Marketplace available for selling. New items have 1-7 day 
   trading lock (prevents RMT abuse).

Q: What's the best class?
A: No "best" class. Warrior = tanky, Mage = high damage, 
   Rogue = skill-based. Pick what suits YOUR playstyle.

Q: Do I need a guild?
A: Recommended but optional. Guilds give +5-30% bonuses, 
   social features, access to guild dungeons. Joinable anytime.

Q: How's the community?
A: Generally friendly but competitive PvP players exist. 
   Global chat monitored for toxicity. Mute/block available.

Q: Can I refund cosmetics?
A: Cosmetics are permanent purchase. Returns within 7 days 
   if unopened (unused). No refunds on used cosmetics.

Q: What happens if I quit then return?
A: Your character preserved permanently. Can rejoin anytime. 
   Level/items kept. No decay for absence.

Q: Are there cheaters?
A: Yes, but rare. Anti-cheat active, bans issued. Report 
   suspects via /report command. Developers actively monitor.

Q: What's endgame like?
A: Mythic dungeons (1x/week, 120+ min clears), PvP ladder, 
   Guild wars, cosmetic collecting, speedrun challenges.
```
