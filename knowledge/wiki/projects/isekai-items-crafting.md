```

  Cost: 30G | Time: 2 phút | Success Rate: 95%
```

## 🎯 ENCHANTMENT SYSTEM

### Enchantment Levels (0-10)
```
Level 0: Base Item
Level +1: +10% Stats (+50G)
Level +3: +30% Stats (+200G)
Level +5: +50% Stats + Special Effect (+500G)
Level +7: +75% Stats + Unique Passive (+1500G)
Level +10: +100% Stats + Power Surge (+3000G)
```

### Enchantment Failure & Enhancement
```
Lvl 0→+1: 100% Success
Lvl +1→+3: 95% Success (5% = Item destroyed)
Lvl +3→+5: 85% Success
Lvl +5→+7: 70% Success
Lvl +7→+10: 50% Success

Recovery Stone: Restore item if fail (300G)
```

### Special Enchantments
```
Attribute Enchant:
  - Fire: +30% Fire damage, immunity
  - Ice: +30% Freeze chance, slow enemies
  - Lightning: +25% ATK, chain damage

Stat Enchant:
  - Warrior's Fortune: +50% ATK
  - Guardian's Blessing: +50% DEF
  - Swift Shadow: +50% SPD
  - Mana Well: +100% Mana capacity
```

## 📌 PRO TIPS

✅ **Farming Strategy**
- Farm enemies 5+ levels below you = Safe & Profitable
- Boss farming = 5x normal drop rate (but harder)
- Group farming = +50% drop rate share
- Night farming = +25% rare drops

✅ **Crafting Optimization**
- Buy materials wholesale (-20% bulk discount)
- Craft at busy times (NPC mood boost)
- Use recipe books for -30% material cost
- Bundle crafting = 3+ items at once (-15% fee)

✅ **Equipment Tips**
- Upgrade weapons first (70% damage boost)
- Armor at lvl 20+ (survival > damage)
- Keep backup gear for different content
- Enchant only +5 items (cost-effective)

✅ **Economy Tips**
- Sell drops quickly (price drops -10%/hour)
- Buy equipment during maintenance (prices low)
- Invest in rare materials (price increase over time)
- Join guilds for discount shops (-30%)
